# Creator Measure

Turn “this worked” into a question the available evidence can answer.

1. Choose one primary objective and comparison. Separate exposure/packaging from consumption and downstream satisfaction or action.
2. Record format, length, audience/source mix, observation window, sample size, and changes made during the run. Read [experiments](references/experiments.md).
3. State a hypothesis and stopping rule before inspecting results. Use randomized comparisons where possible. Treat sequential posts as observational unless assignment supports a causal comparison.
4. Inspect the actual artifact around drops or spikes before explaining them. Mark missing analytics and plausible confounds.
5. Return findings, uncertainty, and one next test. Keep unsuccessful experiments in the bank.

Never invent retention curves, CTR, audience demographics, or significance. Do not combine unlike Shorts and long-form metrics. Without analytics, offer a measurement plan rather than a performance conclusion.
