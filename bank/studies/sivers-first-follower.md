# sivers-first-follower

Source: https://sive.rs/ff — Derek Sivers, “First Follower: Leadership Lessons from a Dancing Guy,” published 2010-02-11. Inspected 2026-10-19: creator-published accompanying text only. Video, delivery, edit rhythm, analytics, and present-day popularity were not inspected. Positions below refer to text progression, not timestamps. No source transcript is redistributed.

**Observation:** The opening offers a complete social event within a short scope. Participants arrive in stages, making the change easy to follow. The conclusion reallocates importance from the initiating person to the first participant who joins. The example precedes the broader advice.

**Interpretation:** The visible sequence described by the text gives the argument a concrete spine; the ending changes how the earlier actions are understood. That is more specific than an abstract list of leadership traits.

**Hypothesis:** A design tutorial may feel clearer when one observable event carries its argument before a final reframing. This is not evidence that the structure caused retention or reach.

**Transfer test:** Write an original account of a team adopting an unfamiliar workflow. Compare an abstract-advice version with an event-first version; ask blind readers to explain what changed and which version they would finish.

**Failure case:** Do not force one anecdote into proof of a universal social law. Missing evidence: footage alignment, audience response, comparable works, and actual behavioral outcomes.
