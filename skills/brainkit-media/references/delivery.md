# Edit and delivery

Lock meaning before polishing transitions. Assemble story beats, temp voice and essential visuals. Inspect a rough cut, revise pacing, then finish sound, captions, grade, and exports. A change to the script can invalidate shot timing, subtitles, and music cues.

Use actual project requirements: frame size, frame rate, color space, audio format, captions, platform overlays, thumbnail, and destination. Verify technical settings with the renderer and destination's current documentation. Keep source assets and intermediate renders separate from delivery files.

Review at normal speed with sound, then inspect important frames and captions. Check the opening against the title/thumbnail promise, continuity, legibility, pronunciation, music masking, accidental silence, export boundaries, attribution, and missing assets. Open the actual exported file; a timeline preview does not prove export success.

Deliver a main export, requested variants, captions if needed, thumbnail/cover, source/provenance manifest, and known limitations. Include alternative aspect ratios only when requested or useful; reframing requires inspection rather than blindly cropping.
