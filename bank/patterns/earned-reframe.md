# earned-reframe

Status: craft hypothesis. Seed evidence: `bank/studies/sivers-first-follower.md`. No retention effect measured.

Use when an audience's likely first interpretation is plausible but incomplete. Show concrete evidence, then reveal a detail that changes its meaning. The ending should make the opening more intelligible.

Original invented example: A team celebrates a faster render. Later it discovers every revision now takes twice as long. The story tests what “faster” should mean across the whole workflow.

Avoid fabricated surprise, withholding basic context, or announcing a reversal the evidence does not support. Test against a straightforward explanation. Score whether people can state the revised understanding; do not score merely whether they noticed a twist.
