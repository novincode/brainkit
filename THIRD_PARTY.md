# Source and reuse policy

The skill instructions and examples here are newly written. Research links acknowledge influences; they are not vendored skill text. No private AtomCut source or reference text is included.

Before copying an outside skill, pin its commit, inspect the specific file's license, preserve required notices, record modifications, and validate the installed result. A repository's visibility or popularity does not grant reuse rights. Track imported components here before merging them.

Public study records store links and short original analysis rather than full third-party scripts, captions, video, or audio. Raw material belongs in `local/` with its acquisition and rights notes. Publication of a dataset requires an explicit redistribution review. This is a contribution policy, not a claim that all analysis requires permission.

Current vendored third-party components: none. Generated starter metadata used the local skill initializer; no initializer implementation is distributed.
