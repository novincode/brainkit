# Sampling and bias

Start small: five creator families × six videos = thirty studies. Suggested families: experiments/builds, visual explainers, personal documentary, comic observation, product/design education. Pick creators only after confirming access to suitable material.

Within a family, include strong and ordinary performers from similar topic, duration, publication period, and format. Record the selection rule and snapshot date. A channel's recent within-format median can provide context; subscriber totals and lifetime views cannot normalize exposure accurately.

Hold out creators or topics for transfer tests. Do not evaluate a new rule only on the examples used to invent it. Record missing videos and unavailable analytics to reveal selection bias.

Do not assign causality to views, comments, spikes, or a guessed emotion. Recommendation exposure, existing audience, topic demand, packaging, timing, and external traffic may explain results. Retention spikes can represent confusion or rewatching; their meaning requires inspection.
