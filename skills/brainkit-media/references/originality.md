# Originality without random novelty

Before prompting, list the first three obvious images or phrases for the topic. Identify why they are predictable. Keep one only if it is unusually useful; otherwise find a specific object, action, contradiction, or observation from the brief.

Replace “innovation” illustrated by a glowing brain with a real change someone can see. Replace generic futuristic interfaces with a concrete interaction. Replace empty inspirational speech with a situation and a choice. These are editing moves, not absolute bans on a visual style.

Check whether the chosen metaphor explains the claim or merely looks clever. Does the detail belong to this project? Could the same asset be attached to fifty unrelated products without changing anything? If yes, improve its specificity.

Avoid forced mistakes, fake handwritten texture, compulsory profanity, or random asymmetry as shortcuts to “human.” Use intentional choices and coherent craft. Capture exact creator feedback and test it on another subject before treating it as a rule.
