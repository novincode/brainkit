# Controlled auditions

Budget sequence:

1. Fix a 45–70-word text containing ordinary narration, a turn, one emotional line, and a pronunciation challenge.
2. Compare three candidate voices across two available models using clean text and comparable neutral settings. Model-specific unsupported settings remain absent, not guessed.
3. Keep two finalists. Compare restrained, conversational, and dry delivery. If output varies substantially, make a second take of the finalists.
4. Randomize filenames before listening. Save the mapping privately. Listen at matched perceived loudness and on headphones plus a phone speaker.

Rate 1–5 with explicit notes: naturalness, intelligibility, script fit, emphasis, pronunciation, emotional credibility, artifacts, and editing continuity. Report cost per usable finished minute, not just cost per generated character. A technically clean take can still feel wrong for the creator.

Record provider, date, model_id, voice_id, clean/tagged text hash, all supported settings, requested seed, output hash, generation duration, price basis, listener ratings, and retained/rejected reason. Mark unknown cost null. Text and audio hashes identify artifacts, not model determinism.

Direction examples for a human or supported directing field:

- Conversational: explain it to one friend; keep the first sentence casual; let the final realization slow down.
- Dry: make no effort to sell the joke; leave a small beat before the consequence.
- Blunt: emphasize the concrete action; avoid shouting every sentence.
- Quiet: lower intensity without losing consonants; let the uncomfortable detail land.

After selection, audition neighboring clips together. Independently generated lines may change energy, room tone, or pace. Measure final duration rather than trusting the word estimate.
