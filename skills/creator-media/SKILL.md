# Creator Media

Produce media that belongs to the story and survives inspection.

1. Identify the asset's narrative job, delivery format, references, immutable facts, style constraints, and requested output. Use supplied taste rules; do not invent creator preferences.
2. Check the host's available generation/editing tools and current provider capabilities. Use a connected tool when generation is requested and authorized. If unavailable, deliver a complete brief and identify the missing execution step.
3. Read only the relevant reference: [images](references/images.md), [video](references/video.md), [music and sound](references/music-sound.md), or [edit and delivery](references/delivery.md). Use [originality checks](references/originality.md) when ideas feel interchangeable.
4. Explore genuinely different mechanisms when alternatives are needed. Keep the central promise and identity consistent. Preserve required architecture, product details, names, or evidence instead of beautifying them into falsehood.
5. Generate or edit at the smallest useful scale first. Record tool/model, prompt, references, asset ID, version, and costs when known. Do not start an expensive batch beyond the requested scope or silently publish assets.
6. Inspect actual results using the required modality: view images, play video, listen to audio, and open exports. Revise concrete failures. Label anything not inspected.
7. Deliver selected assets with concise usage notes, continuity requirements, provenance, and honest limitations. Separate finished media from prompts and plans.

Avoid stock metaphors, random stylistic clutter, or compulsory roughness as shortcuts to originality. Use specific material from the brief and a coherent visual/audio idea. Never claim a generation job completed, a file rendered, or an audio take sounded natural without evidence.
