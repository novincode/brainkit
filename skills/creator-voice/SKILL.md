# Creator Voice

Find delivery that serves the story, not the most theatrical voice.

1. Freeze a short script excerpt and its language. Identify the emotional turn, audience, accent needs, and words that must be pronounced correctly.
2. Separate model, voice identity, synthesis settings, performance direction, and post-processing. Read [auditions](references/auditions.md) for a comparison plan.
3. Choose a neutral baseline. Select candidate voices by actual samples where accessible; otherwise provide selection criteria and state that no listening happened.
4. Verify provider/model support before adding tags or parameters. For ElevenLabs, read [provider notes](references/elevenlabs.md). Keep a clean spoken-text version separate from tagged text.
5. Change one factor at a time, record the run, and compare blind where practical. Check artifacts, meaning, pronunciation, and whether the emotional turn sounds earned.
6. Return a production-ready clean script, a separately marked provider-specific prompt, and an audition plan or actual listening findings. Identify generation and listening work that remains undone.

Do not embed directing prose in spoken text unless it is valid supported syntax. Do not call paid generation by default, invent voice IDs, imitate a real person's identity without authorization, or claim a seed guarantees identical output. A script that reads well still needs an actual audio audition.
