# Revision by function

Generic: “In today's fast-paced world, storytelling is more important than ever.”

Concrete: “The chart was right. The room still got the wrong idea.”

The second creates a situation to explain. It is only usable as fact when supported; otherwise label the example fictional.

Repetitive: “You need to start. Stop waiting. Take action. Begin today.”

Specific: “Record the first sentence before you change the font again.”

Overwritten: “Unlock your authentic creative potential.”

Speakable: “Tell the part you normally leave out.”

Revision order: claim accuracy → changed understanding → missing context → repeated meaning → spoken rhythm → word choice. Cutting every connecting word can destroy clarity. A long sentence that builds toward a turn may work better than six mechanical fragments.

Keep an unusual phrase if the creator actually likes it. Do not use an AI-detector score as a substitute for listening and editorial judgment.
